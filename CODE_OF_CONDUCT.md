# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, experience levels, and approaches.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination,
publication of private information, sexualized language or attention, and
other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
contact channel listed by the repository maintainers. Do not include secrets
or personal scam-message content in a report unless it is necessary to explain
the issue; redact it where possible.

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code and may
take any other action they consider appropriate to protect participants.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
